About Baby Cost Calculator

Baby Cost Calculator is a simple planning tool that helps expecting and new parents estimate common first-year baby expenses.

The calculator includes categories such as diapers, feeding, childcare, healthcare, clothes, nursery setup, and baby gear. Every family is different, so the tool is designed as a starting point that can be adjusted with your own numbers.

The goal is to make early baby budgeting feel less overwhelming and easier to talk through before costs arrive.
